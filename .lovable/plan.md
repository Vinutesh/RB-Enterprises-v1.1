

# Buildio* — Architecture & Interior Design Website

A sleek, dark-themed single-page website for an architecture and interior design agency, inspired by the Buildio Framer template.

---

## 1. Navigation & Layout
- Fixed top navbar with **Buildio*** logo, navigation links (About, Services, Projects, Testimonials), and a "Contact us" CTA button
- Smooth scroll navigation to each section
- Dark background (#0a0a0a) with white text throughout
- Fully responsive for mobile and desktop

## 2. Hero Section
- Large bold headline: "Crafting spaces that match your style and needs"
- Subtitle badge: "#1 in Smart, Stylish Spaces"
- Supporting paragraph text
- Two CTA buttons: "Get in touch" (outlined) and "View services" (filled)
- Hero image of a luxury property with floating overlay badges ("Smart planning", "Seamless process", "Client satisfaction" with 5-star rating and avatar group)
- Scrolling logo ticker strip below the hero

## 3. Past Work Image Carousel
- Horizontally auto-scrolling gallery of circular project images
- Continuous infinite scroll animation

## 4. About / Who We Are Section
- Section header with "Who we are" label
- Description paragraph about the team
- Stats row with animated counters: Years of experience, Team members, Completed projects, Industry awards
- Three value propositions listed (Balance of style and function, Timeless results, Thoughtful process)
- Supporting images alongside text

## 5. Services Section
- Section header: "Services that shape spaces"
- 4 service cards in a grid layout, each with:
  - Service icon
  - Service name (Architectural Design, Interior Design, Space Planning, Space Decoration)
  - Short description
  - Background image
  - "View in detail" link

## 6. Recent Projects Section
- Section header: "A Showcase of our recent projects"
- 3 featured project cards, each with:
  - Project image
  - Project title, detailed description
  - Category tag and location
  - "View in detail" link
- "View all projects" button at the bottom

## 7. Testimonials Section
- Section header: "Hear from our clients"
- Horizontally auto-scrolling testimonial cards
- Each card: quote text, client photo, client name, and service type
- 4 unique testimonials cycling through

## 8. Team Section
- Section header: "The dedicated team behind the craft"
- 3 team member cards with photo, name, and role

## 9. FAQ Section
- Accordion-style FAQ with 5 questions
- Expandable/collapsible answers

## 10. CTA / Footer Section
- Final call-to-action: "Let's create a space you'll love"
- "Get a free quote!" button
- Footer with basic links and branding

---

## Design Details
- **Color scheme**: Dark background with white/light gray text, subtle accent colors
- **Typography**: Bold, modern sans-serif headings with large display sizes
- **Animations**: Smooth scroll, auto-scrolling carousels, hover effects on cards
- **Images**: High-quality placeholder images from Unsplash for architecture/interiors
